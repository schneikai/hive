Hive — Redesign Directions

Five standalone mockups of the main Hive window, all rooted in the orca design language: neutral chrome, Geist type, 36px titlebar, color reserved for status. Direction 1 is a faithful port; the rest each add one twist. Use the chip in the corner of each page to flip between them.

DIRECTION 01 Orca faithful Near-identical port of orca's system: #0a0a0a canvas, lifted #2a2a2a workspace sidebar, 7%-white hairline borders, yellow stepped working spinner, glass overlays. DIRECTION 02 Honey hive accent Same neutral bones, warmed a few degrees, with a single honey-amber brand accent on active states and the send button — orca's restraint, Hive's identity. DIRECTION 03 Porcelain light Orca's real light tokens: white canvas, #f5f5f5 sidebar, ink text, whisper-grey borders — with a dark terminal panel for contrast. DIRECTION 04 Mist soft glass A bridge from today's glass-dark: cool slate surfaces, faint atmospheric gradient, blurred translucent panels, softer radii, periwinkle accent. DIRECTION 05 Mono terminal-grade The sharpest cut: flat graphite, 4px radii, square status marks, mono-uppercase chrome labels, hairline rules — engineered density.
